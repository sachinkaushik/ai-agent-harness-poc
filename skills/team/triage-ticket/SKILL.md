# Triage a support ticket

1. Retrieve the customer's tickets with ticket_lookup and their account with customer_lookup. Cite ticket and customer IDs.
2. Summarize the observed symptom, severity and status. Separate the symptom from possible causes.
3. Recommend one bounded next check and name the owning team from the ticket record.
4. Mark every unverified hypothesis explicitly. Do not claim a fix has been applied.
5. Finish with: Evidence, Likely causes (unverified), Next check, Owner, Open questions. Save to /work/triage.md only when requested and a writable workspace is available.
